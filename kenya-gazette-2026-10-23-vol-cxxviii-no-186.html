GAZETTE NOTICE NO. 16727 PRACTICE DIRECTIONS FOR THE INTERNATIONAL AND ORGANISED CRIMES DIVISION (IOCD) OF THE HIGH COURT IN EXERCISE of the powers under section 5 of the Judicial Service Act, and section 16 of the High Court (Organization and Administration) Act, it is notified for the information of the general public that in the interest of the effective case management and expeditious disposal of cases in the International and Organised Crimes Division (IOCD) of the High Court, the Chief Justice makes the following practice directions: Interpretation In these Practice Directions, unless the context requires otherwise: “Division” means the International and Organised Crimes Division of the High Court as established through a Gazette notice; “Judge” means a Judge of the Division or any Judge designated by the Chief Justice; Application 1. These Directions shall apply to the International and Organised Crimes Division (IOCD) of the High Court of Kenya. 2. All new cases relating to international and organized crimes (as delineated herein) be filed in the Principal Registry of the Division for hearing and determination. The Overriding Objective of Proceedings in the International and Organised Crimes Division (IOCD) of the High Court 3. In the exercise of its authority and jurisdiction, the International and Organised Crimes Division (IOCD) of the High Court shall at all stages of any hearing be guided by Article 159 of the Constitution so as to facilitate the: (a) Just; (b) Expeditious; (c) Proportionate; and (d) Accessible adjudication of all disputes related to international and organized crimes 4. For the purpose of furthering the overriding objective, the court shall handle all matters presented before it to achieve the: (a) Just determination of the proceedings; (b) Efficient use of the available administrative resources; (c) Timely disposal of proceedings; (d) Use of appropriate technology; (e) Safety of witnesses; Scope of Mandate 5. The following matters shall be handled by the International and Organised Crimes Division (IOCD) of the High Court: (a) Original jurisdiction over core international crimes as defined under the International Crimes Act, 2008; (b) All criminal reviews, revisions, appeals and any other applications arising from decisions of trial Magistrates in terrorism, human trafficking and smuggling, kidnapping, arms trafficking, drug trafficking, piracy, wildlife and environmental crimes, and any other cases where the Chief Justice in consultation with the Principal Judge of the High Court allocates to the Division; (c) Petitions and Judicial Review Applications on claims of infringement or the threatened infringement of constitutional rights relating to matters arising from adjudication of crimes within the Division’s jurisdiction; (d) The performance of the duties of a national Judge under the International Crimes Act including the taking of evidence and production of evidence under sections 78 and 79 and the International Crimes (Procedures for Obtaining Evidence) Rules, 2010; (e) Any other matters relating to international and organized crimes under the following Acts: (i) The International Crimes Act; (ii) Extradition (Commonwealth Countries) Act, Cap. 77; (iii) Extradition (Contigous and Foreign Countries) Act, Cap 76; (iv) Prevention of Organised Crimes Act, Cap 59. (v) Mutual Legal Assistance Act, Cap. 75A; (vi) Regional and International Treaties and Conventions on international and organized crimes; (vii) Any other enabling provisions of law. 6. All part-heard cases relating to international and organized crimes pending before other Stations or Divisions of the High Court at the date of publication of these Directions shall continue to be heard and determined by the aforementioned stations or divisions. 7. Subordinate Courts shall continue to hear and determine all cases relating to international and organized crimes in which they have the requisite jurisdiction. 8. Where a matter has been wrongly filed at the International and Organised Crimes Division Registry, it shall be transferred to the appropriate court. The Court on its own motion or upon application by either of the parties shall have the discretion to order for the transfer of such matters. Rules of Procedure 9. The Division shall apply rules of procedure and evidence applicable to criminal trials in Kenya as well as the International Criminal Court Rules of Procedure and Evidence, where applicable. 10. Where no express provision is made under any written law, the Court shall adopt such other procedure as it considers appropriate in all the circumstances and having regard to the rights and views of the parties. Case Management and Pre-Trial proceedings 11. All cases shall be filed electronically through the e-filing system. 12. Classified and confidential documents shall be filed through a secure portal established under the registry. Conduct of Hearings 13. The exercise of the Court’s original jurisdiction under the International Crimes Act shall be by a single Judge of the Division. 14. The trial court shall conduct a status conference within 14 days of the filing of a new case to set a timetable for the trial. 217 THE KENYA GAZETTE 23rd October, 2026 52485248 15. The trial court shall set rules for disclosure and issue any directions for protective safeguards for witnesses and evidence upon the conduct of the status conference. 16. The Court shall conduct other pre-trial conferences as necessary for the efficient management of the trial including directions on days for the trial, number of witnesses, registry updates on courtroom infrastructure required and other support measures, use of video-link testimonies from secure locations in case of protected witnesses and procedures for presentation of digital and electronic evidence. Reviews, revision, appeals and other applications 17. Reviews, revisions and any petition or judicial review application shall be heard by a single judge of the Division; 18. An appeal from the subordinate court shall be heard by a single judge of the Division, except where in any particular case, the Presiding Judge of the Division directs that the appeal shall be heard by two judges of the Division. Witness and Victim Protection 19. The Court shall order measures for the protection of witnesses and victims including in camera hearings, voice distortion, opaque screens and confidentiality of records as necessary. 20. The Court shall issue directions for victim participation including structured legal representation where necessary Other Safety and Security Measures 21. The Court shall issue directions on the safety and security measures necessary in each case upon assessment of any security concerns that may be brought to its attention by the Deputy Registrar in coordination with the relevant members of the Court Users’ Committee. Court Users’ Committee 22. The Division shall have a court users’ committee (CUC) which shall include the Presiding Judge as the Chairperson, judges of the Division, heads of subordinate courts with jurisdiction to try offences within the jurisdiction of the Division and the Deputy Registrar of the Division who shall be the secretary. Other members of the CUC shall be appointed in accordance with the National Council on Administration of Justice CUC Guidelines. 23. The Committee may co-opt any person to be a member of the committee. 24. The Committee shall be an advisory body to the Division. 25. The committee shall meet at least once every quarter. 26. The committee shall regulate its own procedure. Miscellaneous These Directions shall come into force upon publication. Dated the 19th October, 2026. MARTHA K. KOOME, Chief Justice and President of the Supreme Court of Kenya.
