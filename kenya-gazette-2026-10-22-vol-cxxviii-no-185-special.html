GAZETTE NOTICE NO. 16720 THE CONSTITUTION OF KENYA THE COUNTY GOVERNMENTS ACT (Cap. 265) COUNTY ASSEMBLY OF BUSIA COUNTY ASSEMBLY OF BUSIA STANDING ORDERS SPECIAL SITTING OF THE COUNTY ASSEMBLY PURSUANT to Standing Order No. 27 (3) of the County Assembly of Busia Standing Orders, it is notified for the information of Members of the County Assembly of Busia and the general public that there shall be a special sitting of the County Assembly to be held on Friday, 23rd October, 2026, at 9.00 a.m. at the Busia County Assembly Buildings. The business to be transacted at the special sitting shall Tabling of the Busia County Budget Review and Outlook Paper for FY 2025/2026. In accordance with Standing Order 27 (4) of the County Assembly of Busia Standing Orders, the business specified in this notice shall be the only business before the County Assembly during the special sitting, Dated the 21st October, 2026. FREDRICK W. ODILO, Speaker, County Assembly of Busia.

GAZETTE NOTICE NO. 16721 THE CONSTITUTION OF KENYA THE COUNTY GOVERNMENTS ACT (Cap. 265) COUNTY ASSEMBLY OF KIRINYAGA COUNTY ASSEMBLY OF KIRINYAGA STANDING ORDERS SPECIAL SITTING OF THE COUNTY ASSEMBLY NOTICE is given to all Members of County Assembly of Kirinyaga that pursuant to Standing Order No. 18 (1) and (2) of the County Assembly of Kirinyaga Standing Orders, on the request of the Governor, I have appointed Tuesday, 3rd November, 2026 at 11.00 a.m., as a day for special sitting of the County Assembly to be held in the County Assembly Chambers, Kerugoya. The Business to be transacted during the special sitting shall be the Annual Address by the Governor, Anne Mumbi Waiguru. In accordance with Standing Order No. 26 (3) of the County Assembly of Kirinyaga Standing Orders, the business specified shall be the only agenda before the County Assembly. Dated the 14th October, 2026. MUTETI MURIMI, Speaker, County Assembly of Kirinyaga. PRINTED AND PUBLISHED BY THE GOVERNMENT PRINTER, NAIROBI
